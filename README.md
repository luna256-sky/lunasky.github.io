##Data scientist
**A data scientist is a person who uses programming,math,statistics and data to solve business problems and provide and insight on business to help improve.

##They begin by
-Generating an **idea** 
-Ensuring that the**data** is available
-They will the begin the **research** in the model to see if it works
-They the **analyse** the idea to see wether it is improving the model
They then **ship**the idea for the production code
-The model at this point will be ready to get **sent out**

##who they associate with
**They mostly associate with the product manager,data engineer,data analysist and software engineers so as to be able to effectivly solve the problems in the businnes with the right data and support from manager .

##Structure
**There are tow types of structures for data scientists that is **embedded** and **in house consultancy**

##**Embedded**
**It consists on a group of data scientists,software engineers and analysists who are given a single purpose or problem to slove.

##**In house consultancy**
** It consists on a group of data scientists,software engineers and analysists who are given wide range of problems to slove not just a single domain.
